# Product vision

DataVirgo is Alexandre's astrology practice online.

Consultations come first. Someone writes via the contact form. There is no booking or payment on the site. The public chart tool comes later, and stays behind `features.chartTool` until that launch.

The site ships in English. Portuguese is a later locale, not a rewrite.

The blog is the Substack publication. The site indexes it and links through.
